The Last Seed

Humanity has lost every forest. One final seed remains. Can a single seed restore life to Earth? Begin the journey and discover the answer.

The Beginning

Beneath a world that had forgotten the sound of birds and the whisper of trees, one final seed slept in silence. It carried within it the memory of every forest that had ever lived, waiting for the day hope would return.

The Dying Earth

Years of greed had turned oceans into dust and forests into memories. The skies grew darker each season until the Earth itself seemed to stop breathing.

The First Drop

Then, against all odds, a single drop of rain fell from the silent sky. It touched the forgotten seed, awakening a promise that nature had never truly surrendered.

The New Forest

From one tiny seed came roots, branches, and endless life. The wind carried fresh leaves across the land as rivers flowed once more and the Earth remembered how to bloom.

A New Beginning

Every great future begins with one small act of hope. The Last Seed was never just about saving a tree—it was about reminding humanity that even after the darkest night, life can begin again.
